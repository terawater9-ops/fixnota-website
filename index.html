Fixnota clips to your shirt. While you look at the furnace, the leak or the panel, just say what you see. Before you're back in the truck, the quote, the work order and the parts list are written and ready to send.

How it works

  1. Clip it on

    One button. Press it when you walk in, press it again when you leave. No phone in your hand.

  2. Talk while you work

    Describe the problem, the fix and the parts the way you'd tell a helper. English, Arabic, Spanish, Punjabi and more.

  3. Check it and send

    Your quote is built from your own price list. Tap approve and it goes to the customer by text or email.
